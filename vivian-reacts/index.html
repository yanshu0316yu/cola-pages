Who we are

You keep hearing that China is a huge opportunity — but everything you read is either corporate jargon, outdated, or written in Chinese.

vivian.reacts is a boutique consumer-insight consultancy founded by Vivian, a strategist based in Beijing. We work at the layer most reports miss: how young Chinese people actually decide, what they're proud of buying, which channels they trust — and where overseas brands get it wrong.

We deliberately stay small and opinionated: every engagement gets founder-level attention, honest judgments, and recommendations you can act on. Legal, tax, and company setup are better left to licensed professionals — our value lives in culture, consumers, and communication.

  • 🏙️Beijing-based — we see this market every day with our own eyes, not through translated reports.
  • 🗣️English-first — built for global teams, from people who read both sides daily.
  • 🎯Boutique by design — small on purpose: senior attention on every project, no junior hand-offs.
